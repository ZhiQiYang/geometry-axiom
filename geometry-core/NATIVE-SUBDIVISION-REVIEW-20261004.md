Consequently every intermediate result has at most `16 + 8*16 = 144` intervals; the conservative `32*(8+1) = 288` buffers are sufficient. Every production guide/grid clip caller and its corresponding output capacity was audited. Per-loop polygon buffers correctly remain 32. Existing stagger band/selection budgets remain explicitly 32; sample transport says `exact:false`.

Exact rejects excess and reports required counts; sample retains whole cells, original identities, stated stride and completeness. It validates the returned sampled cells, as explicitly stated in [the engineering contract](../geometry-core/GRID-SUBDIVISION-NATIVE-CONTRACT.md#L31). Count/preflight occurs before writing result JSON; query reads and failures preserve history/pending state.
